CLEAN(ish)

Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Clean(ish) collects, why, and your rights. It covers the website cleanish.app and the Clean(ish) app, which launches on 1 January 2027. Until then, the only personal data we collect is waitlist email addresses. US residents can also read our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

Who we are

Clean(ish) is run by Bouchface Labs Limited, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17379658), registered office 2 Frederick Street, Kings Cross, London, WC1X 0ND, United Kingdom. We are the "controller" of your personal data. Contact us at hello@cleanish.app.

The short version

  • What you log in the app (treats, drinks and plants) is health data. We only process it with your explicit consent, and you can withdraw that consent and delete everything at any time.
  • If you don't create an account, your logs stay on your phone.
  • We never sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • Flora, our AI helper, receives only what it needs to reply, never your name or email.

Waitlist (website)

If you join the waitlist we collect your email address so we can tell you when the app launches. Our legal basis is your consent. We send one launch email and then delete the list within 3 months of launch, unless you've separately agreed to hear more. Sign-ups are collected by our form provider, Formspree, Inc. (USA). The website is hosted by GitHub, Inc. (USA), which, like any web host, receives visitors' IP addresses to deliver the pages. The website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking, and its fonts are served from our own site.

Health data and your consent

Information about what you eat and drink can reveal things about your health, so we treat all your logs as health data (special category data under UK and EU data protection law, and consumer health data under some US state laws). Our legal basis is your explicit consent, which you give when you set up the app. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your data in the app or emailing us; this doesn't affect processing that happened before you withdrew.

Other legal bases

  • Contract: running your account and subscription.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the service secure and preventing abuse (for example, daily limits on Flora).
  • Legal obligation: keeping records we're required to keep.

Flora (AI helper)

Flora is an AI, not a person, and not a dietitian or doctor. When you message Flora, your message and limited context are sent through our server to our AI provider (Anthropic, PBC, USA) to generate a reply. We never send your name or email. Our provider does not use these messages to train its models. Don't share information with Flora that you wouldn't want processed this way.

Who we share data with

We never sell personal data or share it for advertising. We use a small number of service providers to run Clean(ish):

Now (website):

  • GitHub, Inc. (USA): hosts the website.
  • Formspree, Inc. (USA): collects waitlist sign-ups.

These providers process data under their own terms of service and privacy policies.

From the app's launch:

  • Apple and Google: app distribution, subscriptions and payments. We never see your card details.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (USA): subscription status.
  • Supabase, Inc.: account login and backup database, stored in the UK or EU (when accounts are introduced).
  • Anthropic, PBC (USA): AI replies for Flora (when Flora is introduced).

Before any app data reaches these providers, we will have data processing agreements in place that require them to protect your data and use it only on our instructions. We will disclose data if required by law.

International transfers

Some providers are based in the USA, so data may be transferred outside the UK and EU. Before app data is transferred, we will put approved safeguards in place, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and its UK extension where the provider is certified.

How long we keep data

  • App data on your phone: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Account and backup data: until you delete your account, then removed from our systems within 30 days (and from backups within 90 days).
  • Flora conversations: not stored by us beyond what's needed to deliver the reply and enforce daily limits.
  • Waitlist emails: deleted within 3 months of launch.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent. You can delete all your data directly in the app (Settings). For anything else email hello@cleanish.app; we'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority. We'd appreciate the chance to sort it out first.

If you live in the United States

Your logs are "consumer health data" under laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act. We collect it only to provide the app, with your consent; we don't sell it or share it for advertising; and you can access or delete it at any time as described above.

Security

The website is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and access to our accounts is protected by two-factor authentication. In the app, data will be encrypted in transit and each account will only be able to access its own data.

Age

Clean(ish) is for adults aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Changes

If we make important changes we'll tell you in the app before they take effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.

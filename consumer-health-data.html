Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy is for residents of US states with consumer health data laws, such as Washington's My Health My Data Act and Nevada's similar law. It sits alongside our main privacy policy. Clean(ish) is run by Bouchface Labs Limited (company number 17379658), 2 Frederick Street, Kings Cross, London, WC1X 0ND, United Kingdom.

Consumer health data we collect

From the app's launch on 1 January 2027, we collect what you choose to log: food exceptions (sweet treats, savoury snacks, fast food and takeaways, sugary drinks), alcoholic drinks, the plants you eat, the dates and times you logged them, and your weekly allowances and goals. We treat all of this as consumer health data. The website collects no consumer health data.

Where it comes from

Only from you, when you use the app.

Why we collect and use it

Only to provide the app's features to you: your allowance, streak, alignment gauges, stats, trophies and, if you use it, Flora. We don't use it for advertising and we don't sell it.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or share consumer health data with third parties for their own use. We only use service providers that process it on our behalf to run the app, under data processing agreements, and only when you use the related feature:

  • Supabase, Inc.: backup and sync, if you create an account.
  • Anthropic, PBC: AI replies, if you use Flora (only the context Flora needs, never your name or email).

We may disclose data if required by law.

Your consent and rights

We collect consumer health data only with your consent, which you give when setting up the app. You can:

  • Access the consumer health data we hold about you, and the list of providers it has been shared with.
  • Delete it, at any time, in the app (Settings) or by email.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time.

To exercise these rights, use the app's settings or email hello@cleanish.app. We'll respond within 45 days. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision, and we'll respond to your appeal within 45 days. If you're unhappy with the result, you can contact your state Attorney General.

What counts as one?

One portion is one tap. Only five kinds of thing count:

  • Sweet treats: a slice of cake, a bar of chocolate, 2 scoops of ice cream or 4 biscuits
  • Savoury snacks: 1 small bag of crisps
  • Fast food and takeaways: 1 burger, 1 side or 2 slices of pizza
  • Sugary drinks: 1 can or 1 frappé
  • Alcohol: 1 pint, 1 small glass of wine or 1 single. A large glass, a double or most cocktails count as 2.

Portions are strict on purpose: a burger and fries is 2, and a whole takeaway pizza is about 4. (Sadly, a whole pizza isn't one.)

Everyday foods never count, so there's no need to log them: bread, cereal, baked beans, pasta sauce, ketchup or hot sauce, the milk in your tea. Clean(ish) counts treats. It doesn't count calories, weigh anything or read ingredients.

What's the difference between my budget and my ideal?

Your budget is how many treats and drinks you allow yourself this week, from 0 to 7 each. It resets every Monday, and your Dash shows what's left.

Your ideal is your north star: the week you're working towards over the longer term. It can be the same as your budget, lower or higher.

  • Change next week's budget in Settings any time until Sunday night. This week's budget never changes mid-week.
  • Your streak follows your budget. Your gauges follow your ideal. So raising your budget can protect your streak, but it won't move your gauges.

How do the gauges work?

You have a gauge for food, alcohol (if you track it) and plants. Each one holds your last 4 weeks up against your ideal. The needle runs from Aligned (green) to Not aligned (red), and the label shows which way it's heading: On course, Drifting or Stable.

  • They move slowly, on purpose. One treat won't swing them; a pattern will. A one-off shows straight away and wears off.
  • A habit above your ideal keeps drifting, faster the further over it is. Getting back to your ideal brings the needle back.
  • New to Clean(ish)? Your gauges appear after your first 24 hours, and read Calibrating for your first 7 days. Everyone starts at Aligned.
  • Changed your ideal? The new one counts from that day, and "Adjusting to your new ideal" shows for 4 weeks. Raising your ideal never turns a gauge green straight away.
  • Plants gauge: compares the different plants you've had in each week with your weekly target.
  • Want the reason behind yours? Tap "Ask Flora why" under a gauge.

The gauges are part of Premium.

How does my streak work?

Your streak is the live timer on your Dash. It keeps running while you're within your budget: a treat doesn't break it. It restarts the moment a treat takes food or drinks over that week's budget.

  • Your personal best (PB), your longest streak so far, shows under the timer.
  • Logged a treat for an earlier time? The streak is worked out from that time.
  • Logged something by mistake? Tap Undo straight away, or remove any log from the last 7 days in the Mindful exceptions card or Settings.

How do plants work?

Every different plant you eat counts once a week: veg, fruit, beans, grains, nuts, herbs and spices. Log them after eating, in the Plants tab of + Log. Each one sticks into this week's sticker book, which starts fresh every Monday. Your Dash shows how close you are to your weekly target.

  • Counts: whole plants, herbs and spices included.
  • Doesn't count: juice, white bread, white pasta, crisps, popcorn, coffee, tea, dark chocolate, or anything that's already a treat tile.
  • Can't find a plant? Search for it, or add it as your own plant.
  • Eat the same plants often? Save them as a meal, then add them all with one tap.
  • Stuck one by mistake? Tap its sticker to remove it from this week.

Logging plants is free. The plants gauge is part of Premium.

What is Flora?

Flora is an AI guide, using AI from Anthropic, for Premium members. Flora knows your week (your budget, gauges and plants), so it can help when a craving hits, suggest a meal from what's in your kitchen, put a shopping list together, or explain your gauges and stats.

  • What Flora sees: what you type, a summary of your week, your notes if memory is on, and any foods you've asked it never to suggest. Never your name or email.
  • Memory: Flora can keep a few short notes on your phone about what helps. See or delete them any time in Settings → Flora → What Flora remembers, or turn memory off.
  • Foods Flora never suggests: set them in Settings → Flora. The app checks every reply, but Flora can't promise a recipe or product is safe for an allergy, so always check the label.
  • Your chat stays on your phone. Delete it any time in Settings → Flora → Delete chat history. Our server passes messages through and stores none of them.
  • Flora never changes your logs and never messages you first. You can send up to 60 messages a day.

Flora isn't a doctor, dietitian, therapist or emergency service.

How do I cancel? Can I get a refund?

Apple and Google handle Clean(ish) subscriptions, so you cancel with them:

  • In the app: Settings → Subscription → Manage subscription.
  • iPhone: Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Clean(ish) → Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Google Play → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Clean(ish) → Cancel.

Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial ends or your subscription renews, so you're not charged. Deleting the app or your Clean(ish) account doesn't cancel a subscription.

Refunds are decided by Apple and Google. On iPhone, ask at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, ask in Google Play; if that doesn't work, email us.

On the founding price? It's yours for as long as you stay subscribed. If you cancel and come back later, you'll pay the normal price.

How do I restore my purchase?

On a new phone, or after reinstalling: sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account you subscribed with. Then open Clean(ish) and tap Settings → Subscription → Restore purchases.

Still showing Free? Email us and say whether you use iPhone or Android. Please don't send card details.

Your data

Everything you log is yours. It lives on your phone and is included in your phone's own backup (iCloud Backup on iPhone, Google backup on Android). Setting up a new phone from that backup brings it back.

  • Download my data (Settings → Your data) saves one file with everything: logs, plants, budgets, ideals, settings, saved meals, and Flora's notes and chat.
  • Start fresh clears your logs, streak, personal best, gauges and trophies, and starts you again. It keeps your account, subscription, settings, saved meals and own plants. It can't be undone, so you can download your data first.
  • Delete my account and data permanently deletes everything, on your phone and on our servers. It doesn't cancel a subscription: do that with Apple or Google.

Worth knowing: deleting the app on an iPhone deletes its data. Data doesn't move between iPhone and Android. Nothing is saved if your phone's backups are off.

Privacy

  • Your logs stay on your phone and in your phone's own backup.
  • Flora gets a summary of your week, never your full logs, your name or your email. Our server stores no chat content, ever, and your messages aren't used to train AI models.
  • Lock-screen notifications never show what you've logged.
  • When you tap Contact us or Send feedback, the email includes your app version and phone model, never anything you've logged.

The full details are in our privacy policy.

Taking a break

If tracking ever starts to feel stressful, it's fine to take a break or stop.
